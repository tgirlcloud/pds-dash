migrating to tgirl.cloud

A short guide on how to transfer your bsky.social account (or other PDS) to the tgirl.cloud PDS.

  1. Open pdsmoover.
  2. Fill out your current handle, email and password in the appropriate fields.
  3. Enter https://pds.tgirl.cloud as the new PDS.
  4. Set your new handle as your username followed by .tgirl.beauty.
  5. Generate an invite code below and paste it into pdsmoover.
  6. You may see that pdsmoover wants a verification code, but you can ignore!
  7. Press the Moove button and you're all done.

Congratulations on moving to the tgirl.cloud PDS!
